✨ About RGB 2026

Rhythm Game Bash (RGB) is the ultimate festival celebrating rhythm games, community, and competition! Whether you are a casual player looking to enjoy the artist alley and interactive booths, or a hardcore competitor aiming for the podium on the Main Tournament Stage, there is something here for everyone.

Join us for two incredible days of high-BPM action, DJ sets, exclusive merch, and the best rhythm game community in the region!
